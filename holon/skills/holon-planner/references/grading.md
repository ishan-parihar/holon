# Grading a plan

| Grade | Meaning | Typical approver |
|---|---|---|
| G0 | routine, reversible, inside the working directory, done many times | head (earned), verified on a sample |
| G1 | minor: small reversible change | head or CEO |
| G2 | significant: several files or a shared system, or a new kind of work | CEO or Chair |
| G3 | major: wide blast radius, hard to undo | Chair unless authority was earned |
| G4 | critical or irreversible: deletes data, spends money, speaks publicly | Chair |

The organization computes a floor from objective signals (risk, external effects, sensitive
paths, writes outside the working directory, decomposition, fewer than 3 verified successes
in the task class). Your grade can raise the floor, never lower it. Under-grading is caught by
the floor and by audits, and it costs the trust that lets your department approve its own work.
