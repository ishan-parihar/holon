# Grades: the canonical definition

| Grade | Meaning | Typical approver |
|---|---|---|
| G0 | routine: reversible, inside the working directory, done many times | head (earned); verified on a sample once trusted |
| G1 | minor: a small reversible change | head or CEO |
| G2 | significant: several files, a shared system, or a new kind of work | CEO or Chair |
| G3 | major: wide blast radius, hard to undo | Chair unless authority was earned |
| G4 | critical or irreversible: deletes data, spends money, speaks publicly | Chair |

The effective grade is the maximum of the grade you propose and an objective floor computed
from: the work's risk; external effects (G2); writes outside the working directory (G2) or to
sensitive paths (G3); decomposition (G2); and novelty, fewer than three verified successes in
the task class (G2). You can raise the floor, never lower it. The plan then goes to the lowest
approver whose authority covers the grade.
